Process

Six steps. Two are yours.

Each step is run by one agent. The whole thing is run by a person who answers for the result.

  1. You · 5 min

    Tell us what you need

    The form below or straight to chat. Product, goal, deadline and budget.

  2. Concierge · instantly

    Completes the brief

    Asks about whatever is missing, writes up the brief and sends you a confirmation that we understood each other.

  3. Creative · within 24 h

    Prepares a proposal

    Moodboard, script, rough budget and a list of what we will need from you.

  4. You · 15 min

    Approve or adjust

    A call or comments right in the proposal. Nothing gets filmed until you say yes.

  5. Dispatcher · instantly

    Splits up the work

    Shoot, edit, AI generation, web and graphics. Every task has a name and a deadline.

  6. A person · days

    Shoots and delivers

    You see versions as we go. After delivery we check in once to see how the video is performing.
